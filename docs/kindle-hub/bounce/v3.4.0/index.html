01 / MOTION & INTELLIGENCE

Eight independent trajectories share one room. Each ball has its own harmonic pitch and collision timing. Drag or flick any active ball. Muted balls are frozen and never collide with active balls.

02 / TUNED IMPACT & ATMOSPHERE

ABOUT THE ROOM / SOUND ENGINE

Each ball has its own fixed musical note, unchanged whether it hits a wall or another active ball. Impact speed changes note velocity and brightness. The balls gradually choose slightly different bounce angles and play chords when they collide, keeping music changing without teleporting. Audio runs through an original tuned mallet oscillator, stereo tape delay, then post-delay convolution reverb. This is not a copy of a commercial pedal.
