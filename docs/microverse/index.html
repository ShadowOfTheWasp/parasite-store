MICROVERSE

THREE CASSETTES. FORWARD OR REVERSE. SAME PARASITE DNA AS ORBIT.

REC · REVERSE · SPEED
02 / TAPE B
REC · REVERSE · ECHO

Each cassette starts empty. Record up to five seconds through the microphone or import a WAV. Press PLAY ALL to loop your tapes. Reverse any cassette independently while the other two carry on. Watch the reels turn, then twist speed, tone, level and echo. Global DARK, WOBBLE, DIRT and SPACE controls make it beautifully unstable. All audio stays on the tablet.
