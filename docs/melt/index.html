MELT_

FOUR BALLS. FOUR WALLS. MAGNETIC CHOIR ECHO.

THE SOUND DIVES IN. THE WALL SENDS IT BACK.

Four moving voices bounce between tape heads and movable walls. Change each wall to alter the time between echoes, and change each ball to alter its voice. Original Mellotron-like synthetic choir tones dissolve into a wobbly, warm tape echo, with decay, tone and flutter controls. Entirely offline after installation.
